# Session-Specific Warm-Ups
Owner: Training Content / Sports Science (02) · v1.0 · Wed Nov 18, 2026
Status: **Draft for founder approval.** Founder direction (11/18): **every workout has a warm-up relevant to that day's session.** Applies to Approach-generated **and** coach-built sessions, volleyball and trainer mode. Replaces the fixed W1–W3 warm-ups in `general-library.md` §6.
Uses exercises from `drill-library.md`, `general-library.md`, and `exercise-catalog-v3.md` (movement prep). Program context: `content/program-design.md` §12.

---

## 1. Structure: RAMP
Every warm-up has three parts, generated from what's in the session:
| Part | Purpose | Time | Picked from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R · Raise** | Raise temperature and heart rate; rehearse rhythm | 2–3 min | Movement-prep drills (§3a) |
| **A · Activate & mobilize** | Prepare the joints and muscles the session loads most | 3–5 min | Pattern pools (§3b), based on the session's primary patterns |
| **P · Potentiate** | Build up to the session's first block: easy versions of the day's power work at rising intent | 2–3 min | The day's A-block (§3c) |
Then, inside the session: **ramp-up sets** before each loaded tier A or P exercise (§4).

**Total:** off-season 10–12 min · preseason 10 · in-season 8 · post-season 10 · home 6–8. Counts toward the session estimate. When a session is fit to a shorter time (`program-design.md` §4a), the warm-up drops to its **compact version**: one Raise round (2 drills), 3–4 Activate items, one Potentiate step; minimum 6 min (5 min in-season). It's never removed by fitting.

## 2. How the generator reads a session
1. **Primary patterns** = the patterns of the exercises in blocks A–C (for a coach-built session: the first 4 strength or power exercises in the order the coach wrote them).
2. **Session type** from those patterns:
   - **Lower: squat** (squat is the first strength pattern) · **Lower: hinge** · **Lower: single-leg/lateral** · **Upper: push** · **Upper: pull** · **Total body** (both lower and upper in A–C) · **Power-only** (A-block is most of the session).
3. **A-block type:** Olympic derivative · vertical/reactive plyo · lateral plyo · throws · none.
4. Build R + A + P from the tables below. **Rotate** drills across the week (pick the next items in each pool) so the warm-up doesn't repeat exactly every session.
5. **Equipment:** only items that fit the session's equipment (band items fall back to their bodyweight options). **Limited space** setting (team or session): Raise uses in-place drills only.

## 3. Pools
### 3a. Raise (choose 3–4; 2 × 10 yd each, or the in-place version)
| Drill | In-place version (limited space) |
|---|---|
| `high-knees` | High knees in place, 15 s |
| `a-skip` | A-skip in place, 15 s |
| `lateral-shuffle` | 3 shuffles each way × 3 |
| `carioca` | 3 steps each way × 3 |
| `walking-knee-hug` | Standing knee hug, 5 each |
| `walking-quad-stretch` | Standing quad stretch, 5 each |
| Easy cardio (trainer mode, from the gym profile): bike, rower, treadmill, elliptical | 3 min, Easy |
Volleyball sessions use drills (athletes move together, no machines). Trainer mode starts with 2–3 min of easy cardio, then 2 drills.

### 3b. Activate & mobilize (choose 4–5 from the session type's pool; 1 set each)
| Session type | Pool (reps) |
|---|---|
| **Lower: squat** | `ankle-rock` 8 each · `hip-90-90` 5 each · `worlds-greatest` 3 each · `band-lateral-walk` 10 each way · `glute-bridge` 8 each · `bodyweight-squat` 8 (pause 2 s at the bottom) |
| **Lower: hinge** | `leg-swings` 8 each direction · `inchworm` 4 · `cat-camel` 6 · `glute-bridge` 8 each · `bird-dog` 4 each · `band-hamstring` 20 s each |
| **Lower: single-leg / lateral** | `lateral-lunge` (no weight) 5 each · `short-lever-copenhagen` 15 s each · `band-lateral-walk` 10 each way · `ankle-balance` 4 each · `hip-flexor-stretch` 20 s each · `walking-knee-hug` 5 each |
| **Upper: push** | `arm-circles` 10 each way · `scap-pushup` 8 · `push-up-plus` 6 · `thoracic-rotation` 5 each · `band-pull-apart` 15 · `wall-slide` 6 |
| **Upper: pull** | `wall-slide` 6 · `lat-reach` 20 s each · `band-er` 12 each · `thoracic-rotation` 5 each · `cat-camel` 6 · `er-90-90-iso` 15 s each (light) |
| **Total body** | Two from the lower pool that matches the first lower pattern + two from the matching upper pool + `inchworm` 4 |
| **Power-only / preseason power** | `worlds-greatest` 3 each · `ankle-rock` 8 each · `leg-swings` 8 each direction · `inchworm` 4 · `band-lateral-walk` 10 each way |
**Position add-on (volleyball):** one extra item from the position's durability focus. Hitters and opposites: `band-er` or `er-90-90-iso` · Middles: `ankle-rock` or `soleus-calf-raise` 10 · Setters: `wrist-roller` (light) or `wall-slide` · Liberos/DS: `short-lever-copenhagen` or `lateral-lunge`.

### 3c. Potentiate (by A-block type; all submaximal, rising effort)
| A-block | Potentiation |
|---|---|
| **Olympic derivative** | Empty-bar complex, 2 rounds: RDL × 5 → hang high pull × 5 → front squat × 5 (or with a PVC/dowel). Then the lift's ramp-up sets (§4). |
| **Vertical / reactive plyo** | `pogo` 2 × 10 → `cmj` × 3 at about 75% → 2 reps of the first plyo at about 75% |
| **Lateral plyo** | `skater-hop` 3 each at about 75% → `line-hops` side-to-side × 20 → 2 reps of the first plyo at about 75% |
| **Throws** | 2 light throws of each throw in the session, building to about 80% |
| **None (strength-first or upper day)** | Lower: `cmj` × 3 easy → `bodyweight-squat` × 5 fast. Upper: `pushup` × 6 → `mb-chest-pass` × 3 light (if a med ball fits) |
| **In-season** | Same as above, but one set only and stop at 80% effort |

## 4. Ramp-up sets before heavy lifts
Generated for each **loaded tier A or P exercise** (from the working weight the athlete is about to use). Logged as warm-up, never counted as work sets, never used for weight suggestions.
| Working weight (first work set) | Ramp-up sets |
|---|---|
| ≤ 95 lb (or dumbbell lifts) | 1 set: about 50% × 5 |
| 95–185 lb | Empty bar × 8 · 50% × 5 · 70% × 3 |
| > 185 lb | Empty bar × 8 · 40% × 5 · 60% × 3 · 75% × 2 · 85% × 1 (only if the work sets are at 85%+ of e1RM) |
- Round to the nearest 5 lb. Rest 60–90 s between ramp-up sets.
- **Olympic derivatives:** empty bar × 5 · 50% × 3 · 70% × 2, then work sets.
- **Second heavy lift in the same session** (e.g., squat then bench): ramp-ups for the new lift; skip the bar × 8 if it's the same pattern.
- **Testing days:** the testing warm-up (`programs/testing-day-checklist.md` Part 2) and §4a ladders replace this. Unchanged.

## 5. Special sessions
| Session | Warm-up |
|---|---|
| **Home session** | Raise: in-place drills only (2 min). Activate: 3 items from the matching pool that need no equipment. Potentiate: only if the session has jumps (pogo 2 × 10). 6–8 min. |
| **Light home day, extra recovery session** | No separate warm-up (the session is the warm-up). |
| **Testing day** | Unchanged (testing-day checklist Part 2). |
| **Stations session** | Warm-up is done together before Part 1, built from Part 1 (power + primary) and the most common pattern across stations. |
| **Deload week** | Same warm-up; potentiation stops at about 70%. |
| **Trainer mode (adults)** | Same system. Raise starts with 2–3 min of easy cardio from the gym profile, then 2 drills. Replaces W1–W3. |

## 6. Coach control (per `program-design.md` §10)
- The warm-up is **generated by default** for every session, Approach-built or coach-built, and regenerates when the coach changes the session.
- The coach can **edit** any item, **swap** items, **reorder**, **add** their own, **save it as a template** ("Our lower warm-up"), or **remove** the warm-up. Once edited, it stops auto-regenerating for that session (with a "Regenerate" button).
- A team setting **"Default warm-up: Approach / My template / None."**

## 7. Data (for Claude Code)
- `Session.warmup?: { raise: ProgramExercise[]; activate: ProgramExercise[]; potentiate: ProgramExercise[]; edited?: boolean }`.
- `buildWarmup(session, ctx)`: pure function using §2–§3; `ctx` includes phase, position (for a group session, the group's position; for mixed groups, skip the position add-on), equipment, limited space, home/gym.
- Ramp-up sets: `rampSetsFor(exercise, workingWeight, ctx)` per §4, shown before the first work set; stored in the log as `warmup: true`.
- Athlete view: a **Warm-up** card at the top of the session with the three parts, each item linked to its how-to; one **Done** checkbox per part. Not on boards except the tile state "Warming up."

## 8. Examples
**Off-season Day 1, Lower: squat, Outside Hitter** (A: Hang Power Clean + Depth Drop → Box Jump; B: Back Squat)
- R: `a-skip` · `lateral-shuffle` · `carioca` · `walking-knee-hug` (2 × 10 yd each)
- A: `ankle-rock` 8 each · `hip-90-90` 5 each · `band-lateral-walk` 10 each way · `bodyweight-squat` 8 (pause) · **add-on** `band-er` 12 each
- P: empty-bar complex × 2 rounds → `pogo` 2 × 10 → `cmj` × 3 at 75%
- Then ramp-up sets for Hang Power Clean and Back Squat.

**Off-season Day 4, Upper: pull, Setter** (A: Push Press + MB Overhead Throw; B: Weighted Pull-Up)
- R: `high-knees` · `carioca` · `walking-quad-stretch`
- A: `wall-slide` 6 · `lat-reach` 20 s each · `band-er` 12 each · `thoracic-rotation` 5 each · **add-on** `wrist-roller` light 15
- P: 2 light MB overhead throws building to 80% → Push Press ramp-up sets

**In-season Day 2, Fast** (A: MB Overhead Throw + Pogo; B: Trap Bar Jump) · 8 min
- R: `a-skip` · `lateral-shuffle` (in place if space is limited)
- A: `leg-swings` 8 each direction · `ankle-rock` 8 each · `worlds-greatest` 3 each · `band-lateral-walk` 10 each way
- P: `pogo` 1 × 10 → `cmj` × 2 at 80% → 2 light throws

**Home Lower** (A: none; main: Rear-Foot Elevated Split Squat → bodyweight rung) · 6 min
- R: high knees in place 15 s · A-skip in place 15 s · standing knee hug 5 each
- A: `ankle-rock` 8 each · `glute-bridge` 8 each · `hip-flexor-stretch` 20 s each

**Trainer mode, Upper: push, adult client** · 9 min
- R: bike 3 min Easy · `arm-circles` · `walking-knee-hug`
- A: `scap-pushup` 8 · `thoracic-rotation` 5 each · `band-pull-apart` 15 · `wall-slide` 6
- P: `pushup` × 6 → bench ramp-up sets

## 9. Acceptance criteria (for Product)
- [ ] Every session (generated or coach-built, volleyball or trainer mode, gym or home) shows a Warm-up card with Raise, Activate, and Potentiate built per §2–§3, except light home days, extra recovery, and testing days (which use their own).
- [ ] A squat-first session's Activate items come from the Lower: squat pool; an upper pull session's from the Upper: pull pool; a session with an Olympic derivative gets the empty-bar complex.
- [ ] Ramp-up sets appear before each loaded tier A/P exercise per §4, are logged as warm-up, and never affect weight suggestions.
- [ ] Changing a session's main lift regenerates its warm-up unless the coach edited it; edited warm-ups keep the coach's version and show "Regenerate."
- [ ] Limited space uses in-place Raise drills; home sessions use no-equipment items only.
- [ ] The coach can save a warm-up template and set the team default to Approach / template / none.
