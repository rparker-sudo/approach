## 2. Other things to consider (team side)
1. **Athletes on two teams (high priority).** Many high school players join a club team in winter and spring, and the seasons overlap in November. In the app, an athlete on two teams gets two coach programs, and the G2 caps apply **per program**, so they could lift 6–8 days a week. **Rule I recommend:** one active lift program per athlete. If they're on two teams, they pick which program they follow (the other team's program is view-only), and the caps and the ≥ 1 day off rule apply across everything they do. The coach of the non-active team sees "Following [other team]'s lift program." Product + UX.
2. **Time away from volleyball.** AAP recommends 2–3 months a year away from a single sport (in 1-month blocks). Suggest a **"Break" option** after post-season: 1–4 weeks with only optional extra recovery sessions and no streak or effort-board pressure. Then G9 eases athletes back in. Cheap to add; Product decides.
3. **Most of our athletes are girls** (high school volleyball is overwhelmingly a girls' sport). That's already reflected in the knee/ankle prehab emphasis and landing cues, and Fuel card 1 refers possible under-fueling to an adult. **Don't add menstrual-cycle tracking or anything similar.** It's sensitive health data about minors (Legal would object, D-007).
4. **Coach readiness for testing days.** Supervision confirmation only helps if the coach knows what to watch for. I can write a one-screen **"Before your first testing day" coach checklist** (rack setup, spotting, when to stop a set, warm-up ladder). It's low cost and supports Legal's accepted-risk position. Recommend including it in v2.2.
5. **Effort-board incentives.** Watch soft-launch data for athletes logging extra recovery every day or a drop in "full week" rates after the board launches. Market Research can ask about it in coach interviews.
6. **Conditioning** is still undecided. Volleyball conditioning is mostly short sprints, change of direction, and repeated jumps, and practice already covers a lot of that. My recommendation: leave it out of v2.2, and revisit for the individual phase (off-season athletes training alone are the ones who need it).
