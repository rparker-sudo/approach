3. **Run the room from the board.** Put the live board on a screen: who's lifting, who's resting, who's done. Never weights.

## 6. Safety and privacy (`/safety`)
**`<title>`** Safety and privacy · Approach Strength
**Meta description:** How Approach Strength handles parent consent, testing, team boards, and athlete data.

**H1:** How we handle safety and privacy
**Intro:** Most of our athletes are teenagers. These rules are built into the app, not left to settings.

### Parent consent comes first
- Every athlete under 18 needs a signed parent consent form, recorded by their coach, before they can log anything.
- The form has six separate permissions. Each optional feature (like team boards or testing days) stays off unless the parent agrees to it.
- Athletes under 18 can't change their own permissions.
- A form covers one team. An athlete on a second team needs a second form.

### Training rules for athletes under 18
- No one-rep-max tests. Strength checks are supervised rep-max tests for intermediate and advanced athletes, and the coach confirms supervision that day.
- No testing within 48 hours of a match.
- Effort limits so no one trains to failure, and at least one day a week with no lifting.
- Each athlete gets the version of each exercise that fits their level. If an athlete is on two teams, the lower level wins.
- If an athlete reports pain, the app tells them not to train through it and to talk to their coach, a parent, or an athletic trainer.

### Team boards show effort
Boards show first name and last initial (or initials, or nothing), sessions, streaks, and progress. With parent permission, they can also show jump-test improvement in inches. Never weights, strength-check results, body weight, check-ins, or levels.

### What we collect, and what we don't
- **We collect** what the app needs to work: name, email, birth year, team, position, experience level, workouts, and the check-ins, attendance, and test results a parent has agreed to. Full list in the [Privacy Notice](/privacy).
- **We never collect** location, photos, video, contacts, or full birth dates.
- **No ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.** This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
- **Delete anytime.** Athletes and coaches can delete their account in the app. Data is removed within 30 days. We keep a short record of parent consent and coach supervision for 5 years, with no workout, check-in, or test results, as the Privacy Notice explains.

### What Approach Strength is, and isn't
Approach Strength gives general training education to healthy athletes. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or clear anyone to play. Questions about an injury, pain, or a head injury go to a parent, athletic trainer, or doctor.
