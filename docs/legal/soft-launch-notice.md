## Part A: Privacy Notice
*Last updated: [publish date]*

Approach is a free volleyball strength-training web app in an early test. This notice explains what we collect and why.

**Who can use it.** You must be 13 or older. If you're under 18, your coach must have a signed consent form from your parent or guardian before you can log anything, and each optional feature below stays off unless your parent agreed to it. If you're on more than one team, each team's coach needs their own form. Coaches must be 18 or older. If you're 18 or older, you make these choices yourself in Settings → Privacy.

**What we store on our servers**
- **Account:** name, email, password (stored securely by our login provider, not readable by us), role, volleyball position, birth year, whether you're under 18, and how you want your name shown on boards.
- **Teams:** teams you create or join, your position group, your **experience level** on that team (beginner, intermediate, or advanced, set by your coach), and your coach's posted workouts and calendar.
- **Workouts you log for a team:** exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes, how long the workout took, and whether you hit a personal best. While you're training, your live progress (sets done) is shared with your coach's board and deleted after a day.
- **Daily check-ins** (if allowed): sleep, soreness, energy (1–5) and pain (yes/no).
- **Attendance** (if allowed): practices and matches you attended, and their length.
- **Testing days** (if allowed): jump-test results (including approach touch), strength-check results (weight × reps), and the estimated strength calculated from them; plus a record of the coach confirming supervision.
- **Consent records:** what you or your parent agreed to, when, and which coach recorded it.

**What stays on your phone only:** body weight, personal notes, touch height you log outside a team testing day, and everything in solo mode (no account).

**Who sees it.**
- You and your team's coach see your data. Your experience level is never shown to teammates.
- **On more than one team?** Each coach sees only what you do with **their** team: their team's schedule, workouts from their program, and the choices on their team's form. If you follow another team's lift program, the other coach sees only that you're "following another team's lift program," not which team.
- **Teammates** can see only the team boards: your name as "Jordan M." or initials (or nothing, if you hide), your workout progress, and effort rankings (sessions, consistency, streaks, attendance, hours, jump-test improvement).
- Teammates **never** see your weights, strength-check results, estimated strength, level, check-ins, body weight, or nutrition.
- If you leave a team or a coach removes you, that coach can no longer see your data.

**What we don't do.** No ads. No selling or renting data. No advertising or tracking tools. No location, photos, or contacts. We don't monitor check-ins or notes. If something is wrong, talk to a coach, parent, or athletic trainer in person. If you're struggling, you can call or text 988.

**Nutrition.** The Fuel tab gives general, cited information for healthy athletes. It doesn't use your body weight and isn't individual advice.

**Service providers.** Supabase (database and login) and GitHub Pages (hosts the web app). They process data only to run the app.

**Keeping and deleting.** We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete my account; it's removed within 30 days. Parents can ask for deletion through the coach or the email below. We keep a short **proof record** of consent (who agreed to what, when, and which coach recorded it) and of coach supervision on testing days (team, coach, date, who took part) for 5 years after an account closes. It contains no workout, check-in, or test results, and then it's deleted.

**Questions or requests:** Ryan@thehbcvbc.com

## Part B: Terms (Soft Launch)
*Last updated: [publish date]*

1. **Test version.** Approach is free and in an early test. It may change, have bugs, or stop.
2. **Eligibility.** 13+. Under 18 requires a signed parent/guardian consent form on file with the coach of each team. Coaches are 18+.
3. **Training risk.** Strength and conditioning training, including jump tests and strength checks, can cause injury. Approach gives general training information, **not medical advice**. Learn new lifts with a qualified coach, use weights you can control, never grind out a rep, and stop if something hurts. You (or your parent, for minors) accept these risks.
4. **Coach responsibilities.** Coaches choose and supervise the training they post, and:
   - collect parent consent forms and record exactly what was agreed;
   - **set and confirm each athlete's experience level honestly. Strength checks for minors only unlock at a coach-confirmed intermediate or advanced level;**
   - **personally supervise every testing day, with safety bars or spotters set, before confirming supervision in the app;**
   - stop a set when form breaks;
   - don't use Approach with athletes under 13;
   - **confirm, in writing if you coach for a school, that you're allowed to use Approach with your team;**
   - **don't show the coach board on school- or district-owned screens or networks** unless your school has approved it, and make sure only your team can see it.
5. **Boards.** Team boards rank effort, never strength or bodies. Don't screenshot or share boards outside the team.
6. **Your content.** You own what you log. You let us store it and show it to you, your coach, and (for board items) your teammates, to run the app.
7. **Acceptable use.** No sharing others' data, no harassment, no trying to access other teams' data, no signing or filling in a consent form for someone else.
8. **Ending use.** You can delete your account anytime. We can suspend accounts that break these terms or belong to someone under 13.
9. **Limits.** To the extent the law allows, Approach is provided "as is," and its operator isn't liable for indirect damages. *(Attorney to finalize.)*
10. **Contact:** Ryan@thehbcvbc.com
