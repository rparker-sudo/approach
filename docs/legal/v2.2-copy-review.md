## 3. G4 head-injury line: revised
The spec text said "your school's concussion protocol." That doesn't fit club or adult athletes, and California's return-to-play rule refers to a *licensed health care provider*. **Final wording (Settings → Safety):**

> "If you hit your head or have any signs of a concussion, stop training and tell your coach (and a parent or guardian if you're under 18). Follow your school's or club's concussion protocol. Only a licensed health care provider can clear you to return."

The testing-day checklist line in `testing-day-checklist.md` Part 1 §4 matches this rule. An ATC or sports-medicine review of G3/G4 is still recommended (`finance/quote-requests.md` Q4).

## 4. Q-7 visibility condition (Training chose "lower level")
Using the other team's level inside `resolveSession` and the load-test check is **allowed** (it's like history under compliance § Multiple teams 5: used internally, never shown). Conditions:
1. **No coach ever sees another team's level** or learns that one exists.
2. When an athlete's exercises resolve lower than **this** coach's setting because of another team, this coach's athlete detail lists the swaps under **"Adjusted for this athlete"**, with no reason. (Swaps from this coach's own level keep "adjusted to level.")
3. When a strength check is blocked by another team's beginner setting, the coach sees the existing neutral line, "Not eligible for testing today."
4. The **athlete** may see why, since both teams are theirs: Settings → Program adds "Your exercises follow the lower of your teams' levels."
5. **Acceptance:** Coach A sets intermediate and Coach B sets beginner for a shared athlete. Coach A's view never shows "beginner," Team B, or Coach B (API and UI check), and the athlete gets beginner versions on Team A's program.
