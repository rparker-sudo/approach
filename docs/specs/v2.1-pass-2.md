## P1. Consent becomes six permissions
Pass 1 stored one `consent_status`. Replace it with per-permission flags so each feature turns on per athlete.

1. `team_members` columns (booleans, default false):
   - `c_participation` (box 1)
   - `c_checkins` (box 2)
   - `c_share_checkins` (box 3)
   - `c_boards` (box 4)
   - `c_attendance` (box 5)
   - `c_testing` (box 6)

   Keep `consent_recorded_at` and `consent_recorded_by`. Add `consent_version smallint` (form version; this form = 2).
2. **Migrate pass 1 values:**
   - `training_only` → participation
   - `full` → participation + checkins + share_checkins
   - `pending` → all false
   - `not_required` → see P1.4

   Minors migrated from pass 1 keep what they had. Boxes 4–6 stay off until the coach records the new form.
3. **Coach records consent** with a checklist matching the six boxes (UX F7). Rules: box 3 requires box 2; boxes 2–6 require box 1. The confirm checkbox says "I have a signed parent consent form (version 2) for [name]."
4. **Adults (18+)** consent for themselves in the app. After joining a team (and once for existing adult members), show the "Your choices" screen (UX F8) with toggles for boxes 2–6. Participation is implied by account creation plus the Terms. Default: **check-ins on and shared, boards on, attendance on, testing on**, with a one-line explanation of each. Adults can change any toggle later in Settings → Privacy.
5. **Enforce in RLS:**
   - logs need `c_participation` (or adult)
   - `readiness` needs `c_checkins` and `c_share_checkins`
   - `attendance` needs `c_attendance`
   - `test_results` needs `c_testing`
   - `live_progress` needs `c_boards`
   - board queries return only members with `c_boards`

   Only the coach (minors) or the athlete themselves (adults) can change flags.
6. Settings → Your data lists each permission as on or off.

## P2. Display names on boards (DS-3)
- Default board name is **first name + last initial** ("Jordan M."), derived from `profiles.name`. If the name has one word, use it as is.
- Athlete setting under Settings → Privacy, "Show me on boards as": **Jordan M.** (default) / **Initials** (J.M.) / **Hidden**. Store it as `profiles.board_name_mode` ('short' | 'initials' | 'hidden').
- A minor without `c_boards` never appears on any board, regardless of the setting.
- **Never show a last name in full** on any board.

## P3. Coach board (D-017)
A full-screen, read-only live view for a classroom display or projector.
1. Route `/board/[teamId]` (web-first; works on iPad). Opened from Team → **Open coach board**. Requires the signed-in coach of that team. No public or shareable link.
2. Layout per UX F9: landscape, large type readable at about 6 m, dark theme default.
   - Header: team name, date, today's session name per group.
   - Grid of athlete tiles: board name, status (Not started / In progress / Done), sets done / total, a "PR" badge if a personal best happened today (**no weight shown**).
   - Footer: team goal progress (P4.5) and a rotating effort-board panel (P4) every 20 s.
   - A toggle to hide the effort panel.
3. **Live data:** new table `live_progress` with `team_id`, `user_id`, `day`, `session_id`, `sets_done`, `sets_total`, `status`, `updated_at`.
   - The athlete app upserts it when a set is marked done (debounced 2 s) and on finish.
   - The board subscribes with Supabase realtime.
   - Rows older than 24 h are deleted (scheduled function, or delete-on-read of old days).
   - Add `pr_count smallint` to `workout_logs`, using the finish summary's "new bests" count.
4. **Hard rules (MUST):** the board never shows check-ins, readiness, the pain flag, bodyweight, nutrition, weights or loads, test results, or estimated maxes. Only athletes with `c_boards` (or adults with boards on) and not `Hidden` appear.
5. Demo mode: the board works locally from local logs, without realtime.

## P4. Team effort board (D-018)
Ranks **effort and improvement, not strength.** Shown as a tab on the coach's Team screen, a card in the athlete's Progress tab (their team list plus their own rank), and the coach-board footer.

1. **Metrics** (season to date, plus a "this block" filter):
   - **Sessions:** prescribed lift sessions completed. One log counts per scheduled session per day. Extra or duplicate sessions don't count.
   - **Consistency:** completed ÷ scheduled lift sessions, as %. A session the calendar moved counts as scheduled on its new day.
   - **Week streak:** consecutive weeks with every prescribed session completed. **Rest days never break a streak.** Weeks the coach lightened count normally.
   - **Practices & matches:** attendance count (P5). Shown only for athletes with attendance permission.
   - **Hours:** lift minutes plus attended practice/match minutes, **capped at 3 h per day and 15 h per week** (Training Content rule against overtraining).
   - **Improvement:** % gain in approach touch and broad jump vs. the athlete's own first test this block (P6). Shown only with testing permission. **Never lift numbers.**
2. Each metric is its own ranked list. The default view is Consistency.
3. Ties share a rank. Athletes below the top 10 see their own row pinned at the bottom.
4. **Coach controls:** turn the effort board on/off for the team (default on); turn the coach-board effort panel on/off.
5. **Team goal:** the coach sets a season target of total team sessions (default: athletes × prescribed sessions/week × weeks remaining × 0.8). Show "Team total: 412 / 500 sessions."
6. **MUST NOT rank:** loads, test results for lifts, estimated maxes, bodyweight, nutrition, check-in data. Never rank or show anything for athletes hidden or without consent.
7. Badges (private to the athlete, shown on their Progress tab): "4-week streak," "Block complete," "New touch best." Not shown to teammates. (Folds in backlog I-12.)

## P5. Attendance (for P4)
1. Practices come from the team's usual week (`volleyballWeek` practice days) unless the calendar marks the date otherwise. Matches and tournaments come from calendar events.
2. On those days the athlete's Today screen shows a small "I was there" check (one tap, until the end of the next day). The coach can mark or unmark attendance for anyone from the roster (the coach's entry wins).
3. The coach sets the usual practice length per team (default 120 min) and match/tournament length (default 120 / 240 min).
4. Table `attendance`: `team_id`, `user_id`, `day`, `kind` ('practice'|'match'|'tournament'), `minutes`, `marked_by`. Requires `c_attendance`.
5. Lift minutes: add `duration_min` to `workout_logs`, from the workout's start and finish time, capped at 120.

## P6. Testing days (D-020, DS-5: everything live, coach supervision required)
This restates Training Content's safety-guidelines §4a. Follow it exactly:

1. **Scheduling (coach):** Calendar → Add → **Testing day**, with a battery type of `full` / `jump` / `mid` (jump tests + trap bar 5RM).
   - The app suggests dates per phase (§4a table) but the coach picks.
   - **Block** a testing day within 48 h before a match/tournament ("Testing can't be within 2 days before a match").
   - Add `test` to `CalendarEvent` as `{ date, type, label?, test?: 'full'|'jump'|'mid' }`. The day keeps its underlying day type.
2. **Generator:** on a testing day, that day's lift session for the team/group is replaced by a **Testing session**: warm-up, then tests in the §4a order (power first).
3. **Supervision lock (MUST):**
   - Load tests stay locked for everyone until, on that day, the coach taps **Start strength check** and confirms "I'll be supervising this test, with safeties or spotters set."
   - Record this in `test_sessions` (`team_id`, `group_key`, `day`, `coach_id`, `confirmed_at`, `battery`).
   - Jump tests don't need the lock.
4. **Eligibility for load tests (MUST, checked per athlete):**
   - On a team, with `c_testing`
   - Level intermediate or advanced
   - Not on a solo program
   - Today's check-in isn't pain = yes or "run down" (same rule as `team.tsx`). If the athlete has no check-in permission, ask "Any pain today?" yes/no on the spot, and **don't store it**. Yes → skip.

   Ineligible athletes see only the jump tests, with a neutral note ("Strength checks are for intermediate and advanced athletes" / "Skip strength checks today. Talk to your coach.").
5. **Tests and entry:**
   - **Jump:** approach touch (inches; feeds the existing touch log), broad jump (inches), med-ball overhead throw (feet; the coach sets the ball weight per team, default 6 lb). Best of 3: the athlete enters each attempt and the best one is saved.
   - **Load:** trap bar deadlift 5RM; back squat 5RM (advanced may choose 3RM); bench press 5RM (advanced may choose 3RM); dumbbell gyms use goblet squat or DB bench 8RM.
   - The warm-up ladder is shown per §4a. Up to 3 working attempts, with the rest timer at 3:00.
   - The athlete enters the **last set completed with good form** (weight × reps).
   - On-screen cue: "Your coach stops the set when form breaks. Never grind."
6. **Estimated max:** e1RM = weight × (1 + reps/30).
   - Always label it "Estimated strength: ~245 lb (estimate)." **Never "your max."**
   - Visible only to the athlete and their coach.
7. **Loading from tests:**
   - For a tested lift, the working-weight suggestion is `min(e1RM × phase upper %, progression suggestion)`. If there's no history, use e1RM × phase lower %.
   - Phase ranges: off 70–80%, pre 75–85%, in 70–80%, post 60–70%.
   - The RPE target stays on screen. Rule: if RPE is hit at a lower weight, use the lower weight (the athlete adjusts, and progression follows).
   - Round to 5 lb (barbell) or 2.5 lb (dumbbell).
   - A new test replaces the old estimate, **even if it's lower**. The athlete sees "Updated your starting weights"; the coach sees the change in athlete detail.
   - Untested lifts keep pass 1 progression (G5).
8. **Storage:** table `test_results` (`team_id`, `user_id`, `day`, `test_id`, `value`, `unit`, `reps`, `e1rm`, `entered_by`, `voided bool`). The coach can edit or void a result. Requires `c_testing`. Solo athletes: jump tests only, stored on the device (Progress → "Jump test").
9. **Copy:** "Testing day" / "Strength check." Never "max out." No ranking of lift results anywhere (P3.4, P4.6).

## Acceptance criteria
**Consent**
- [ ] Pass 1 consent values migrate correctly (training_only, full, pending, not_required).
- [ ] Coach checklist enforces box dependencies. Each feature appears per athlete only when its flag is set. RLS rejects attendance, test_results, readiness, and live_progress writes without the flag (API tests).
- [ ] An adult sees the "Your choices" screen once and can change each toggle in Settings.

**Boards**
- [ ] Coach board loads only for that team's coach. Tiles update within about 3 s of a set being marked done on another device.
- [ ] Board and effort board never render weights, test results, e1RM, check-ins, pain, bodyweight, or nutrition (UI review plus a check of the query's select list).
- [ ] Name modes work ("Jordan M." / "J.M." / hidden). A minor without box 4 never appears.
- [ ] Effort metrics: extra sessions don't count; rest days don't break streaks; hours cap at 3/day and 15/week; improvement uses jump tests only.
- [ ] The coach can turn the effort board and the board's effort panel off.

**Testing**
- [ ] A testing day can't be scheduled within 48 h before a match or tournament.
- [ ] Load tests stay locked until the coach confirms supervision that day; `test_sessions` records who and when.
- [ ] Beginners, solo athletes, athletes without box 6, and athletes with pain or "run down" today can't enter load tests; they can enter jump tests (if they have box 6, or are solo on the device).
- [ ] e1RM is computed correctly (e.g., 200 × 5 → 233) and labeled as an estimate everywhere.
- [ ] Working-weight suggestions for tested lifts never exceed e1RM × the phase upper %, and a lower re-test lowers them.
- [ ] No "1RM," "max out," or "your max" strings anywhere.

**Fuel**
- [ ] No grams, ounces, or calorie numbers in Fuel or Today; no body weight step in onboarding; cards show sources and the disclaimer.

**General**
- [ ] `docs/compliance.md` rules all hold; `tsc`, `lint`, and `build:web` pass; version stays 2.1.x; CHANGELOG and `build-snapshot.md` updated; git tag `v2.1` once both passes are done.
