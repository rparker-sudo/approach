# Spec: Approach v2.1, the soft-launch release
Owner: Product Strategy (01), with Legal (07), UX/UI (03), and Training Content (02) · Draft v1.0 · Mon Nov 9, 2026
Source of truth: Approach project → `product/specs/v2.1-soft-launch.md`. This repo copy is a snapshot; don't edit it here.
Decisions: D-013 (volleyball first), D-014 (one Expo app, web first), D-015 (parent-consent soft launch, web only until 2027), D-016 (check-in synced to the coach, pain note removed).

## Goal
Make v2.0 safe to put in front of real high school volleyball athletes **through the web build**, with a signed parent consent form on file before any minor's data reaches the server.

> **Scope change (decision session, 11/9):** v2.1 now also includes the coach board (D-017), effort board (D-018), Fuel phase 1 (D-019), and testing days (D-020), plus safety fixes G1, G2, G5. Build in two passes:
> - **Pass 1 (build now):** S1–S11 below.
> - **Pass 2 (spec coming):** D-017–D-020 and the expanded 6-box consent form. **Don't start pass 2 until this file is updated with it.** Build the S2 consent columns so more consent options can be added later without a rewrite.

## Out of scope for v2.1
Parent email/online consent flow (v2.2+) · App Store submission or iOS-specific work · billing · Android app · readiness-based auto-adjustment · messaging, comments, feeds.

## Hard rules
Read `docs/compliance.md` first. Everything there is a MUST for this release.

## Changes

### S1. Age gate (all users)
1. First screen of onboarding, **before** the role step, for everyone, including solo users with no account: "When were you born?" with an **empty** month/year picker (no default, no hint that 13 is the cutoff). Month + year is enough.
2. Under 13 → neutral block screen: "Sorry, you're not eligible to use Approach right now." Don't save the birth date, name, or email anywhere. Save a local flag (AsyncStorage / localStorage) so reloading and entering a different date shows the block screen again.
3. Choosing **coach** requires age 18+. If under 18, the coach option shows: "Coach accounts are for adults 18 and older."
4. Store only **birth year** and **is_minor** (under 18 at signup). Locally: on the profile. Server: on `profiles` (passed in sign-up metadata and set by `handle_new_user`). Never store the full date.
5. Users who already have a v2.0 account and no birth year on file see the age question once at next launch, before anything else. Under 13 → the account is signed out and a deletion is requested (S5's RPC).

### S2. Parent consent on file (coached minors)
The coach collects signed paper or e-signed copies of `docs/legal/parent-consent-form.md` and records them in the app.

1. Add `consent_status` to `team_members`: `not_required` (adult) · `pending` (minor, default) · `training_only` · `full`. Also `consent_recorded_at` and `consent_recorded_by`.
   - Minors join as `pending`. Adults join as `not_required`.
2. **Coach roster** (`src/app/team/[id].tsx`): each minor shows a badge: "Consent needed" / "Consent: training only" / "Consent: full". Tap a pending athlete → sheet: "Record parent consent for [name]" with two choices that match the form:
   - "Training only (no check-ins)"
   - "Training and check-ins shared with me"

   Confirm text: "I have a signed parent consent form for [name]." The coach can change or clear it later.
3. **Athlete with `pending`**: sees the team program, calendar, and exercise how-to **read-only**. Logging a workout and checking in are disabled, with a banner: "Your coach needs your parent's signed consent form before you can log. Ask your coach for the form." Local workouts aren't sent while pending.
4. **Athlete with `training_only`**: can log workouts. The readiness check-in card is hidden, and nothing is sent to `readiness`.
5. **Athlete with `full` or `not_required`**: everything works as in v2.0.
6. **Enforce on the server** with RLS, not only in the UI:
   - `workout_logs` insert requires the member's status to be in (`training_only`, `full`, `not_required`).
   - `readiness` insert/update requires (`full`, `not_required`).
   - Only the team's coach can update the consent columns. Athletes can't change their own status.
7. The athlete's own profile shows their consent status under Settings → Your data.

### S3. Remove the free-text pain note (D-016)
1. Remove the "Where? (optional)" field from `src/components/ReadinessCard.tsx`.
2. Remove `painNote` / `pain_note` from `src/lib/backend/types.ts`, `supabase.ts`, `local.ts`, and every display (`team/[id].tsx`, `progress.tsx`, ReadinessCard summary).
3. Migration: set existing `pain_note` values to null, then drop the column.
4. Keep the pain yes/no question. Under it, add helper text: "Tell your coach, parent, or athletic trainer in person about any pain."

### S4. Check-in sharing notice
The first time an athlete opens the check-in card (and on the card as small text afterward): "Your coach sees your check-ins. Teammates never do." Store "seen" locally.

### S5. In-app account deletion
1. Settings → Account → "Delete my account" (visible whenever signed in).
2. Confirm dialog explains what's deleted. The user types DELETE to confirm.
   - Coach version also says: "Your teams and every athlete's logs for those teams will be deleted."
3. New RPC `delete_my_account()` (security definer, callable only by `authenticated`, deletes `auth.users` where id = `auth.uid()`; existing `on delete cascade` handles the rest). Then clear local data and return to onboarding.
4. Demo mode (`local.ts`): delete the local account and its teams.

### S6. Coach access ends when an athlete leaves
Today a coach can still read `workout_logs` and `readiness` for an athlete after removing them (RLS checks only `is_team_coach`). Change the select policies so a coach sees rows only for **current members** of their team. The athlete still sees their own rows.

### S7. Legal pages and links
1. Add static pages to `public/` (served by GitHub Pages): `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `consent.html` (printable form). Generate them from `docs/legal/soft-launch-notice.md` and `docs/legal/parent-consent-form.md`. Use the brand theme, plain HTML, no scripts, no trackers.
2. Account step (AccountForm): under the button, "By creating an account you agree to the Terms and Privacy Notice" with both linked.
3. Settings: a "Legal" section linking Privacy, Terms, and the parent consent form. The coach team screen gets a "Print parent consent form" link.
4. Links open in a new tab (web) or the system browser (iOS).

### S11. Training safety fixes (Training Content 02, `content/safety-guidelines.md` G1, G2, G5)
Any other change to `RX`, `REST_BY_ROLE`, `splitFor`, or `MAX_LEVEL` needs Training Content sign-off. These three are approved:
1. **G1, level gate:** in `pickExercise` (`generator.ts`), never return an exercise with `level > maxLevel`. If nothing at the athlete's level fits their equipment, use an easier or bodyweight option at or below their level, or **skip the slot**. Never fall back to a harder exercise.
2. **G2, lift-day caps and rest:**
   - Max lift days: beginner 3, intermediate 4, advanced 5; **in-season 3 for everyone**.
   - Require at least 1 day a week with no lifting or conditioning.
   - Days beyond the cap can only be mobility/recovery sessions.
   - In the day pickers (`week.tsx`, `schedule.tsx`, onboarding, coach team lift days), show "Max 3 lift days for your level. Add mobility days instead." rather than silently disabling.
   - Existing programs over the cap: on next open, offer to rebuild within the cap.
   - Coach team programs use the cap for the team's phase.
3. **G5, progression cap:** in `suggestWeight` (`progression.ts`), cap each increase at about 10% of the last weight, rounded to 2.5 lb, max +10 lb (barbell) or +5 lb (dumbbell/kettlebell). Never suggest weight increases for band-only setups or timed/hold exercises (`band-row`, `band-pulldown`, `band-good-morning`, band setups of `pallof` and `face-pull`, holds).

## Acceptance criteria (Claude Code: check every one)
- [ ] G1: a beginner with only a pull-up bar never gets a level 2–3 exercise; slots with no fitting exercise are skipped.
- [ ] G2: a beginner can't select a 4th lift day (message shown); in-season caps at 3 for everyone; every generated week has ≥ 1 day with no training; extra days are mobility only.
- [ ] G5: after hitting the top of the range with a 10 lb dumbbell, the suggestion is +1 lb rounded to 2.5 lb (i.e., 12.5 lb or less), never +5; band and hold exercises get no weight suggestion.
- [ ] A new user can't reach the role step without entering a month/year of birth; the picker starts empty.
- [ ] A birth date under 13 shows the block screen; nothing is saved except the local block flag; reloading still blocks.
- [ ] Someone under 18 can't create a coach account.
- [ ] Only birth year and is_minor are stored (local profile, `profiles` table); no full date anywhere.
- [ ] A minor who joins a team is `pending`, can view the program, and can't log or check in. A direct API insert into `workout_logs` or `readiness` is rejected by RLS.
- [ ] Coach records "training only" → athlete can log, check-in card hidden, `readiness` insert rejected.
- [ ] Coach records "full" → logging and check-ins work; coach sees check-ins.
- [ ] An athlete can't change their own `consent_status` (API test).
- [ ] An adult athlete joins as `not_required` and everything works.
- [ ] No pain-note field anywhere in the UI, types, backends, or database. The pain yes/no question still works.
- [ ] First check-in shows the "Your coach sees your check-ins" notice.
- [ ] Delete my account works for athlete and coach in Supabase mode and demo mode; the auth user and all their rows are gone.
- [ ] After a coach removes an athlete, the coach can't read that athlete's logs or check-ins (API test).
- [ ] Privacy, Terms, and Consent pages load on the GitHub Pages build and are linked from the account step and Settings.
- [ ] Repo search finds no "injury prevention" or "prevent"/"manage" injury claims in user-facing copy.
- [ ] README, LICENSE, version, CHANGELOG, and build-snapshot updated.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx expo lint` pass; the web build (`npm run build:web`) succeeds.
