## 9. Welcome, log in, and create account: age question on sign-up only (UX F1 v3.2; Ryan 11/14)
**Why:** in 2.2.0 the age question is always the first screen, and "I have an account" is a chip inside the account step. A returning user on a new phone, a cleared browser, or an iPhone home-screen web app that lost its storage must answer the age question and pick a role again just to log in, although their birth year is already on their account. It also gives the marketing site's `sign-in?role=` links a real Log in screen.

**Rule (Legal approved 11/14; compliance § Age now reads "before any account is created or any data is collected"):**
- Ask the age question **only when an account or on-device (solo) profile is being created**. A new account still answers it **before** we collect an email, a name, or anything else (neutral age screen).
- **Logging in to an existing account never asks it.** The account already has `birth_year` and `is_minor`.

```
Welcome (lockup, tagline)
  ├─ [Log in]  (primary)
  │     → Log in: email + password → Sign in
  │         ├─ success, account has a birth year → home (coach: Team · athlete: Today, or Join team if none)
  │         ├─ success, account has NO birth year (pre-2.1 account) → age question once, saved to the account → home
  │         │     (under 13 → sign out + deletion request, as v2.1 S1.5)
  │         └─ error → inline error, stay on the form
  │     link: "New here? Create an account" → Create path
  │
  └─ [Create an account]  (secondary)
        → "When were you born?" (month + year, both empty; Next off until both set)
            ├─ under 13 → Not-eligible screen (local block flag; no Back; reload shows it again)
            └─ 13+ → Role ("I'm an athlete" / "I'm a coach")
                     ├─ coach & under 18 → coach card disabled, caption "Coach accounts are for adults 18 and older."
                     └─ → Account form (sign-up only) + agreement line (Terms, Privacy Notice links)
                          + coach only: Coach Terms checkbox (§8)
                          → athlete: Join team → Name/Position/Level/Week (existing)
                          ghost link "Train solo, no account" (athlete only: after the age question, never before)
```

**Screens:**
- **Welcome:** lockup, then the tagline, then two full-width buttons, each **56 pt**: **Log in** (primary) and **Create an account** (secondary). Nothing else: no age question, no role picker.
- **Log in:** H1 "Log in". Email and Password fields, then **Sign in**. Links (44 pt): "Forgot password?" and "New here? Create an account." Back returns to Welcome. No role picker.
  - **Error:** "That email and password don't match. Try again." (`errorText`, under the button). Fields keep their values.
  - **Loading:** button reads "Signing in…" and is disabled.
- **Create path:** same as 2.2.0, minus the "New account / I have an account" chips. The account form is sign-up only, with one link back: "Already have an account? Log in."
- **Not-eligible screen:** lockup, H1 "Sorry, you're not eligible to use Approach Strength right now." No other actions. Neutral tone; don't mention age. The local block flag also **hides Log in** on that device.
- **Pre-2.1 accounts with no birth year:** after log-in, the full-screen age question appears once before the tabs (the existing `(tabs)/_layout` check).

**Links from the marketing site:**
- `app.approachstrength.com/sign-in?role=coach|athlete` opens the **Log in** screen directly.
- If the visitor taps "Create an account" from there, `role` preselects the role card **after** the age question. The age question still comes first.
- An unknown or missing `role` is ignored.

**When the app shows Welcome again:** after **Log out**, Welcome with Log in first. If a session expires, straight to **Log in**. A signed-in user who reopens the app never sees Welcome, the age question, or the role step.

**Accessibility:** `autoComplete` = `email` and `current-password` on Log in; `new-password` on sign-up.

**Demo mode** (`local.ts`) follows the same flow.

## Appendix B: Level checklist (Training, `content/safety-guidelines.md` §2b; shown read-only behind "See the full checklist")
**Principles:** technique and training history, not strength numbers. Coaches decide; minors never raise their own level. Never based on pain, check-ins, or readiness. The app can suggest later, never decide.

**Beginner (level 1).** Start here if any of these are true: less than about 6 months of regular lifting (2+ sessions a week); not yet meeting the intermediate checks; new to the coach. When in doubt, start at beginner.

**Intermediate (level 2).** Move up when all are true:
1. About 12+ weeks of regular training (in Approach Strength: ≥ 80% of prescribed sessions over the last 12 weeks, or coach-known history).
2. Technique checks, seen by the coach on a normal training day at about RPE 7 (every rep clean, every set):

| Pattern | Check |
|---|---|
| Squat | Goblet squat 3 × 8: hips to knee height or lower, heels down, knees tracking over toes, chest up |
| Hinge | DB Romanian deadlift 3 × 8: flat back, hips push back, weights stay close to the legs |
| Single-leg | Reverse lunge or step-up 3 × 8 each: knee doesn't cave in, balance under control |
| Push | Push-up 3 × 8: straight body, chest to the floor, full lockout |
| Pull | One-arm DB row 3 × 10 each: no torso twist, elbow to hip |
| Core | Plank 45 s with ribs down and no sagging; dead bug 3 × 8 each with the low back down |
| Landing | Squat jump and broad jump, 5 of 5 landings soft and quiet, knees not caving, landing stuck |

3. Coach judgment: follows coaching cues and doesn't chase weight at the expense of form.

**Advanced (level 3).** Move up when all are true:
1. About 2+ years of regular lifting, including 6+ months at intermediate.
2. Barbell technique checks at about RPE 7–8, with safeties set:

| Pattern | Check |
|---|---|
| Squat | Back squat 3 × 5: depth at or below parallel, brace held, bar path over mid-foot, no hips shooting up |
| Hinge | Trap bar deadlift or RDL 3 × 5: neutral spine from floor to lockout |
| Press | Bench press 3 × 5: controlled descent, feet planted, safeties or spotter used without reminders |
| Pull | 3+ strict pull-ups, or inverted row 3 × 10 with a straight body |
| Single-leg | Rear-foot elevated split squat 3 × 8 each, controlled |
| Landing | Lateral bound 5 of 5 each side, landing stuck |

3. Self-management: sets up and uses safeties without reminders and picks weights that match the prescribed RPE.

**Moving down.** The coach can lower a level at any time, for any reason seen in training. Recommended: lower one level after 3+ months without regular lifting. Never lowered automatically, and never because of pain or check-in data.
