## Rules for every pass
- **G-1 Athlete layer (I1):** every session an athlete sees goes through one pure function, `resolveSession(plan, athlete, ctx) → session`. In v2.2 it applies: level (pass 1), effort ceilings (pass 1), return-from-break (pass 1), rest (pass 1), home equipment and per-athlete caps (pass 3). No other code path may build an athlete's session.
- **G-2 History belongs to the athlete (I2):** logs, test results, and progression are keyed to the athlete, not the team. Leaving or switching teams, or going solo, never breaks weight suggestions. **Coaches still only see data created under their own program** (compliance § Multiple teams 5).
- **G-3 Individual data stays local (I7):** anything new that's only for the individual athlete (home equipment, future goals) is stored on the device only.
- **G-4 Boards:** never show weights, loads, RPE, lift test results, estimated maxes, check-ins, pain, bodyweight, nutrition, or **experience level**.
- **G-5 New server fields:** only those listed in this spec (each was reviewed by Legal or is pending as marked).
- **G-6 Copy:** no medical, injury-prevention, treatment, or guaranteed-results claims.

# PASS 1 · Safety and fixes (2.2.0)

## 1.1 Coach-set level and per-athlete resolution (T1, T2, G8)
**Data (Legal approved, compliance § Level):**
- `team_members.level smallint` (1–3, nullable), `level_set_by uuid` (null = self-reported), `level_set_at timestamptz`.
- On join, default to the athlete's self-reported experience with `level_set_by = null`. Backfill existing members the same way.
- RLS: only that team's coach can update the three columns. Athletes can read their own row.

**Coach UI (F24a–c):** roster shows each athlete's level with **"Level not confirmed"** until the coach sets it; setting it writes `level_set_by` = coach and `level_set_at`.

**Athlete UI (F24d):** Settings → Program: "Your level on [team]: Intermediate (set by your coach)" or "(not confirmed yet)."

**Resolution** (`safety-guidelines.md` §2a, all steps): inside `resolveSession`, resolve every exercise in a team program to the athlete's level. Never return an exercise above it; use the easier/bodyweight option or drop the slot; Olympic lifts resolve to a jump below advanced. Keep the coach's sets, reps, and rest.
- Athlete sees **"Your version: Goblet Squat (team: Back Squat)."**
- Logs and `live_progress` store the **resolved** exercise.
- Coach athlete detail shows "3 exercises adjusted to level." The coach can't force a harder exercise on one athlete.

**Load-test eligibility (amends v2.1 P6.4):** for **minors**, load tests require a **coach-confirmed** level ≥ 2 (`level_set_by` = that team's coach). Self-reported levels never unlock load tests. Adults: self-reported is fine.

**Two teams (Q-7, Training Content 11/13; `testing-day-checklist.md` Part 4):**
- **Resolution uses the lower** of the athlete's levels across all their teams (confirmed or self-reported).
- **Minor load tests:** the testing team's coach-confirmed level ≥ 2 **and** no other team has the athlete at level 1 **set by a coach**.
- **Visibility (Legal copy review §4):** no coach sees another team's level. Swaps caused by another team show as **"Adjusted for this athlete"** (no reason). Blocked strength checks show "Not eligible for testing today." The athlete sees "Your exercises follow the lower of your teams' levels."

## 1.2 Multiple teams, one lifting program (TM-2, D-027 data side)
**Data:** `profiles.lift_program_team_id uuid` (nullable; references a team the athlete is a member of). Null = personal program (solo) or, with exactly one team, that team.

**Flows:**
1. Joining a second team → sheet: "You're on 2 teams. Which team's lifting program do you want to follow? The other team's practices and matches still show on your schedule." Default stays the current program.
2. Settings → Teams: switch the program team any time (confirm; takes effect from the next session).
3. Leaving the program team → falls back to the remaining team (if one) or personal program, with a notice.

**Combined schedule (athlete only):** Plan and Today show practices, matches, and tournaments from **all** the athlete's teams, labeled by team name for the athlete. The generator's calendar moves, lift-day caps (R6), the ≥ 1 rest day rule (R5), match-day rules (R7), and the 48-hour testing rule use the **union** of all teams' events.

**Server checks:**
- `can_load_test(user_id, day)`, security definer: returns true or false using all the athlete's team calendars **and the Q-7 level rule (1.1)**. It returns no reason. RLS on `test_results` load rows calls it.
- Logs: `workout_logs.team_id` = the program team at the time of logging. A minor needs that team's `c_participation`. Nothing is written for the other team.

**Coach visibility (compliance § Multiple teams, all of 1–8):**
- Each coach sees only their own team's events, logs created under their program, their own consent-gated data, and their own testing days and attendance.
- The non-active coach's roster shows **"Following another team's lift program"** (no team name).
- Cross-team conflicts show neutral reasons: "Moved: schedule conflict," "Not eligible for testing today."

## 1.3 Coach-set break weeks (TM-3)
- Team calendar → Add → **Break** with a start and end date (`CalendarEvent.type = 'break'`, `endDate`).
- For athletes following that team's program: no lift sessions on break days. Today shows "Break week. Extra recovery is optional." (Extra recovery sessions arrive in pass 2; until then, just the message.)
- A break on a team the athlete **doesn't** follow for lifting doesn't stop their lifting.
- Boards (live in pass 2) treat break days as unscheduled: they don't count against Consistency; a week that's entirely break neither breaks nor extends a streak; break weeks are excluded from the default team-goal calculation.
- G9 (1.4) eases athletes back in after a break of 14+ days.

## 1.4 Return from a break (G9, T9)
- If the athlete has **no logged session for 14+ days** (any program), then for the next **7 days**: strength-slot effort is capped at **RPE 6**, and weight suggestions start about **10% below** the last logged weight for each exercise (rounded per G5, never above the normal suggestion).
- Athlete banner: "Easing back in this week." Coach athlete detail: "Returning from a break: lighter week."
- Applied in `resolveSession`. Uses the athlete's whole history (G-2).

## 1.5 Rest times: tiers, auto rest, floors (T12 without pairing)
Per `rest-and-supersets.md` §2, §3, §5, §6, §7:
- Add `Exercise.tier: 'P'|'A'|'B'|'C'|'D'` for every exercise (§7 tier list, including new exercises in 1.8).
- New pure function `restFor(exercise, prescription, ctx)` replaces `REST_BY_ROLE` as the default. `restSecFor()` keeps "coach setting wins," **with floors.**
- **Floors (MUST):** tier A ≥ 2:00, tier P ≥ 1:30, even if the coach typed less. The builder explains: "Heavy lifts need at least 2:00 rest." (In the **current** builder, F26.)
- Deload and lightened days keep normal rest.
- **Session length estimate** (§5): show "about 45 min" to the coach in the builder and to the athlete on Today (fills `estMinutes`).
- **No supersets in this pass** (`group` and `filler` fields come in pass 3).

## 1.6 Coach effort clamp (T10, D-028)
In the team program builder (the **current** builder, F26), for teams with any minor:

- Power slots may keep "Max effort" (max speed).
- Adult-only teams get the same message as a warning, not a block.
- Backstop: `resolveSession` caps each athlete's strength effort at their level's ceiling (beginner 7, intermediate/advanced 8, in-season 7).

## 3.4 Station rotation (D-030 part 2)

- Station time defaults to fit the longest station with rest floors; a too-short coach time warns and **never** shortens rest below the floors. Coach controls: Rotate now, +1 min.
- Each athlete still gets **their level's version** at every station (G-1).

Never loads or RPE. **Station names are neutral** (no athlete names, levels, or positions); builder helper: "Name stations after equipment, like 'DB bench.'"
- **Live board (extends 2.1):** in Part 2, a station grid showing which rack is at each station and the shared countdown ("Rack 2 · Bench → Rows · 1:40"); tiles unchanged; G-4 still holds.
