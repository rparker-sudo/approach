What Approach is

Approach is an early-version web app for volleyball strength training. Your athlete's coach posts workouts, and your athlete logs them on a phone. It's free; there is nothing to buy. It is in a small test ("soft launch") before a public release.

What information is collected

Stored on Approach's servers, visible only to your athlete and their coach:

  • Name, email address, volleyball position, birth year, and the team they joined
  • Workouts they log (exercises, sets, reps, weights, and any notes they type)
  • Only if you check Box 2 below: a daily check-in (sleep, soreness, and energy on a 1–5 scale, and whether they have any pain: yes or no)

Stays only on your athlete's phone (never sent to us): body weight, jump touch height, and nutrition targets.

Not collected: photos, video, location, contacts, medical or injury details, and no advertising or tracking tools.

Never sold, never used for ads, and never shown to teammates. Approach's operator does not monitor notes or check-ins.

Risks of physical training

Strength, power, and conditioning training carries a risk of injury, including muscle strains, sprains, and more serious injury. Approach provides general training information. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace supervision by a qualified coach. Athletes should learn new lifts with a coach, stop if something hurts, and tell a coach, parent, or athletic trainer about any pain or injury in person. Approach does not watch check-ins for emergencies.

Your choices

Check each box you agree to. Box 1 is required to use Approach. Boxes 2 and 3 are optional; the app works without them.

1. Participation (required). I am the parent or legal guardian of the athlete named above. I agree that they may use Approach under the Approach Terms and Privacy Notice. I understand the risks of physical training described above and accept them for my athlete.

2. Daily check-ins (optional). I consent to my athlete logging the daily check-in (sleep, soreness, energy, pain yes/no).

3. Sharing check-ins with the coach (optional; requires Box 2). I consent to the coach named above seeing my athlete's check-ins.

(For the coach recording this in the app: Box 1 only = "Training only." Boxes 1, 2 and 3 = "Training and check-ins shared with me." Box 2 without Box 3 = "Training only.")

Changing your mind

You can withdraw any consent or ask us to delete your athlete's account and data at any time. Tell the coach, or email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Your athlete can also delete their account in the app (Settings → Delete my account). Deleted data is removed within 30 days.
