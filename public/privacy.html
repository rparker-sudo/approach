Approach is a free volleyball strength-training web app in an early test. This notice explains what we collect and why.

Who can use it. You must be 13 or older. If you're under 18, your coach must have a signed consent form from your parent or guardian before you can log anything. Coaches must be 18 or older.

What we store on our servers

  • Account: name, email, password (stored securely by our login provider, not readable by us), role (athlete or coach), volleyball position, birth year, and whether you're under 18.
  • Teams: the teams you create or join, your position group, your coach's posted workouts and calendar.
  • Workouts you log for a team: exercises, sets, reps, weights, and notes.
  • Daily check-ins, if allowed: sleep, soreness, energy (1–5) and pain (yes/no).
  • Consent records: what your parent agreed to, when, and which coach recorded it.

What stays on your phone only: body weight, touch height, nutrition targets, and everything in solo mode (no account).

Who sees it. You and your team's coach. Never teammates. If you leave a team or a coach removes you, that coach can no longer see your data.

What we don't do. No ads. No selling or renting data. No advertising or tracking tools. No location, photos, or contacts. We don't monitor check-ins or notes. If something is wrong, talk to a coach, parent, or athletic trainer in person. If you're struggling, you can call or text 988.

Service providers. Supabase (database and login) and GitHub Pages (hosts the web app). They process data only to run the app.

Keeping and deleting. We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account anytime in Settings → Delete my account. It's removed within 30 days. Parents can ask for deletion through the coach or the email below. Consent records are kept for 5 years after an account closes, to show consent was given.
