## Judgment calls (for the planning team)
- **Figure captions repeat the alt text.** Copy §8 gives one sentence per image, and the design asks for both alt text and a caption ending "Sample data." With no other approved words, both use the same sentence.
- **Admin preview layout:** design §5.1 puts Training activity across both columns. Following that literally leaves "Parent consent on file" alone on its row at desktop width.
- **Header lockup at phone width:** the plain lockup is 40 px tall (design), and 32 px under 400 px wide, so the Menu button fits at 320 px.
- **Kept from 2.2.1:** dark only (design U1), and the legal pages' light print styles for the printed consent form.
