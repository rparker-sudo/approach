## Judgment calls
- **The coach sees real numbers.** Generated rows store the block's week-1 prescription for the program's level, not a 3 × 5 placeholder. A coach editing one row then gets a session as written with sensible numbers. Athletes' sessions are still prescribed per block and week by `resolveSession`. Plyo rows show the plan's sets × reps; each athlete's contacts are set at resolution.
- **Warm-up minutes** are one decimal in the estimate (Training's 11.5 / 7.7 / 9.3) and whole minutes wherever they're shown.
- **Mobility parts** are rows with roles (breathing and flow = mobility, position care = care, core = core). Rounds are the rows' sets. There's no separate per-part Done button: the session logs like the light day.
- **Moved care work** goes into the first H-Upper / H-Durability / H-Lower E-block in addition to the template's E items (up to 2 extra). Each athlete's app swaps items it can't do at home (e.g., Band ER → Y-T-W without bands).
- **Home prescriptions:** holds use time ("30s each"); Dead Bug is "6 each"; week 3's harder version stays the athlete's choice (the existing suggestion), with tempo 3-1-1 on B otherwise.
- **Reps per set for plyos** are capped in the plyo range (Box Jump 3, Pogo 10). Before this, 2.4.0's per-session budget could write "Box Jump 3 × 23" when it was the only plyo. The §8b position factor (setter −20%, libero/DS −30%) then scales the contacts.
- **One weight-room day** alternates its two §2a sessions week by week from the block start; only the first is on the schedule (no new key).
- **Week-setup coach note** wording: "Care work is on <Day>'s home workout." for every move (Legal's fix), not "Shoulder circuit is on…".
- **Rebuild:** see Product item 1.
